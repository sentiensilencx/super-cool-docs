FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Papyra.

Is Papyra really free?

Yes. Papyra is open source under AGPL-3.0. The hosted service at app.papyra.dev has a free tier, and the software itself costs nothing to self-host forever.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The editor is an offline-first app. Edits are stored locally and synced when you reconnect. Only real-time collaboration requires a connection.

Can I open my existing Word documents?

Yes. Papyra imports .docx (and .odt, .rtf, .html, .md) with high fidelity, including styles, tables, images, headers/footers, comments, and tracked changes.

Where are my documents stored?

On the hosted service, in an encrypted Postgres database in your chosen region. Self-hosted, in your own database and object storage. Anonymous local documents live only in your browser until you sign in or share.

How does this compare to Microsoft Word or Google Docs?

  • vs. Word — no install, real-time collaboration by default, open formats, but Word still wins on macro support and exotic layout features.
  • vs. Google Docs — comparable collaboration, but Papyra is self-hostable and your data never has to leave your infrastructure.

Does it support my language?

The interface ships in 20+ community-translated languages, with full right-to-left support and 80+ spellcheck dictionaries.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Open an issue on the GitHub repository. Security issues should go to security@papyra.dev rather than a public issue.

Can I migrate away later?

Always. Every document exports to .docx/.odt, and self-hosted instances can export the full workspace (documents, comments, users) as a single archive from the admin panel.
