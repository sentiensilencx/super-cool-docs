Quickstart

Go from zero to your first formatted document in about two minutes.

1. Open the editor

Head to app.papyra.dev — or your self-hosted instance URL. No account is required to try the editor; documents are stored locally in your browser until you sign in or share them.

2. Create a document

Click New document on the dashboard, or press N while the dashboard is focused. A blank document opens with the default template (A4 or US Letter, depending on your locale).

3. Write and format

Type as usual. Papyra supports Markdown-style shortcuts as you type:

Type thisTo get
# , ## , ###Heading 1, 2, 3
**bold**Bold text
*italic*Italic text
- itemBulleted list
1. itemNumbered list
> quoteBlock quote
```Code block

You can also use the toolbar or keyboard shortcuts — Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I, and friends all work as expected.

4. Share it

Click Share in the top-right corner, enter email addresses, and pick a role:

  • Viewer — read-only
  • Commenter — can leave comments and suggestions
  • Editor — full edit access

Recipients open the link and start co-editing instantly. See Collaboration for permissions, link sharing, and comment management.

5. Save and export

Papyra autosaves every few seconds. To export, go to File → Download as and pick a format:

.docx   Microsoft Word (best fidelity)
.odt    OpenDocument Text
.pdf    Print-ready PDF
.md     Markdown
.html   Standalone HTML
Tip
Press Ctrl+K in the editor to open the command palette — every menu action, including export, is available from there.
