Formatting text

Character styles, paragraph styles, lists, headings, and direct formatting.

Character formatting

Select text and apply formatting from the toolbar or with shortcuts: Ctrl+B bold, Ctrl+I italic, Ctrl+U underline, Ctrl+Shift+X strikethrough, and Ctrl+Shift+H highlight.

Custom colors are available under the A (text color) and highlighter icons. Colors automatically pass contrast checks and are flagged in the picker when they would be hard to read.

Paragraph styles

The style dropdown in the toolbar applies reusable paragraph styles. Restyling a style restyles every paragraph using it.

Paragraph styles feed the document outline and any auto-generated table of contents.

Lists

  • Bulleted lists — Ctrl+Shift+8; press Tab to nest.
  • Numbered lists — Ctrl+Shift+7; restart or continue numbering from the bullet menu.
  • Checklists — type [] followed by a space, or insert from the Insert menu.

Links and footnotes

Select text and press Ctrl+K to add a link. Papyra auto-detects pasted URLs and offers to hyperlink them. Footnotes are inserted from Insert → Footnote and are numbered automatically.

Clear formatting

Pasting from other apps keeps source formatting by default. Strip it with Ctrl+\ (clear direct formatting) or use Edit → Paste without formatting (Ctrl+Shift+V).

Tip
For long documents, prefer paragraph styles over direct formatting — exports, tables of contents, and restyling all behave much better.
