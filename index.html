Welcome to Papyra

The open-source, browser-based word processor. Write, format, and collaborate on documents from anywhere — no install, no lock-in.

Papyra is a free and open-source alternative to Microsoft Word. It runs entirely in your browser, saves documents in the open .odt and .docx formats, and can be self-hosted on your own infrastructure in minutes.

Note
Papyra is AGPL-3.0 licensed. You can use it, modify it, and host it for anything — including commercial use — as long as modifications stay open.

Why Papyra?

  • Zero install — open a tab and start writing. Works offline with full sync when you reconnect.
  • Real-time collaboration — co-edit documents with live cursors, threaded comments, and suggestions.
  • Open formats — import and export .docx, .odt, .pdf, .html, and Markdown. Your words are never hostage.
  • Self-hostable — a single Docker container or static deployment. Your documents stay on your servers.
  • Extensible — a typed plugin API for custom blocks, toolbars, and export pipelines.
